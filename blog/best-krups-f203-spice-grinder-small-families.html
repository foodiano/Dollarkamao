How I Use It in Foodiano World (The Desi Twist)

On my YouTube channel Foodiano World, fresh masala is the difference between a good curry and a great one — and this is where the desi twist comes in. The twist I love: dry-roast whole cumin, coriander, cardamom, cinnamon and dried red chillies in a pan for a minute until they smell amazing, cool them, then pulse them in the grinder. In under a minute you have garam masala that smells like it came from a spice bazaar, not a warehouse shelf. The same grinder makes chai masala (cardamom, ginger, cloves, cinnamon), crushed red chilli flakes for pizza night, and ground peanuts or sesame for chutneys and desserts.

A tip from buyer reviews: grind in short pulses and shake the bowl between pulses — that is how you get an even powder instead of a fine layer at the bottom and chunks on top. And wipe the bowl and blades clean after every use, buyers say, so yesterday's garam masala does not flavour today's coffee. For a small family, that is the difference between a masala that tastes alive and one that tastes like the jar it came from.
