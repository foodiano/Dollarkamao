FAQ

Are silicone cooking utensils safe for nonstick pans?

Buyers say yes — that is the main reason to buy them. Food-grade silicone heads are soft enough that they will not scratch a nonstick coating, unlike metal spoons and turners. The Umite Chef set is made for nonstick cookware, and buyers say it keeps their pans looking new far longer than metal tools did. Just keep the silicone away from direct flame and follow the 446°F heat limit.

Can silicone utensils melt or warp in a hot pan?

Buyers say the Umite Chef set holds up to 446°F (230°C) without melting or losing its shape, which covers normal frying, simmering and sautéing. The one habit to avoid is resting a silicone spatula on the hot rim of a pan or leaving it sitting in a dry, screaming-hot skillet — buyers say that is how any silicone tool gets damaged. Use it, stir, and rest it on the holder or a plate, not on the pan edge.

How do I clean a silicone utensil set with wooden handles?

Buyers say the silicone heads rinse clean in seconds under running water, and most pieces can go in the dishwasher — but the maker warns against soaking the wooden handles in water for a long time or dishwasher-cleaning them, because wood swells and cracks. The easy routine buyers recommend: hand-wash, dry the handles right away, and hang the set on the included holder. Treat the wood like a wooden spoon and the set lasts for years.
