What to Look For

Gram mode first — cups are the problem, grams are the fix. Buyers say the single most important feature is accurate gram weighing: most modern recipes, and nearly every desi recipe shared online today, list ingredients in grams. A scale that reads in 1-gram steps turns "about a cup of atta" into an exact number you can repeat every single time.

Enough capacity for a real bowl — but slim enough for a drawer. Look for at least 11 pounds (5 kg) of capacity, buyers say, so a mixing bowl of dough or a pot of rice weighs easily. At the same time, the scale itself should be thin and light: buyers love that the Etekcity slides into a drawer and disappears until dinner prep, unlike a bulky machine that eats counter space.

A tare button you can actually use mid-recipe. The tare function zeroes the scale with the bowl on it, so you can add flour, then sugar, then ghee into the same bowl without washing a single measuring cup. Buyers call this the feature that makes the scale a daily tool instead of a baking-day novelty.

A display you can read with a bowl on top. A backlit LCD matters, buyers say, because kitchen light is often dim and a bowl can shadow the numbers. The Etekcity's backlit display is one of the most praised details in its reviews.

Proven reviews (4+ stars, huge volume). A 4.6-star rating across well over 140,000 ratings is not a launch-week spike — it is years of kitchens agreeing. That volume is the best quality check a research-based review can offer.
