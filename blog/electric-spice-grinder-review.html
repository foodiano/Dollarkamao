FAQ

Can I grind coffee beans in a spice grinder?

Yes — most electric grinders including the SHARDOR handle both. Just clean the bowl between coffee and spices (the rice trick above works great) so flavors do not mix.

How long do freshly ground spices stay fresh?

About 2–4 weeks in an airtight jar, versus months of dull flavor from pre-ground jars. Grind small batches weekly for the best taste.

Is a $30 grinder really better than pre-ground spices?

For whole spices like cumin, coriander, and cardamom — absolutely. The aroma difference is immediate. It is the cheapest meaningful upgrade in your kitchen.
