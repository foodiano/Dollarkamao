What to Look For

Small 4-inch plates. This is the whole point of a mini maker. A 4-inch waffle is one perfect portion for a child or a light adult breakfast — and the machine itself is small enough to live in a drawer, not on your counter.

Nonstick surfaces that actually release. Buyers say the best mini makers let waffles slide off with a light grease. If waffles stick, breakfast becomes a chore, so check the reviews for "easy release" before buying.

Fast heat-up, simple controls. The best mini makers have one light and no settings: plug in, wait for the light, pour batter, wait about 3 minutes. No apps, no dials, no learning curve.
