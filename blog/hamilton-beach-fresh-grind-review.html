FAQ

Can I grind coffee beans and spices in the same grinder?

Yes — the Hamilton Beach Fresh Grind handles coffee beans, whole spices, herbs, and seeds. Just clean the removable bowl between coffee and spices (grind a tablespoon of uncooked rice and wipe it out) so flavors do not mix.

Is the Hamilton Beach Fresh Grind loud?

Buyers consistently say it is quieter than competing grinders — one reviewer even uses it without waking sleeping kids. It still makes grinder noise, so grind before the household wakes up if you want to be extra safe.

How much can the Hamilton Beach Fresh Grind grind at once?

The removable stainless steel chamber holds about 4.5 ounces — enough beans for up to 12 cups of coffee. For weekly spice batches, that means one full round of garam masala in a single go.
