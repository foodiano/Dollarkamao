FAQ

Is an oil sprayer really better than pouring oil into an air fryer?

Buyers say yes, and the reason is control: a fine mist coats food evenly with far less oil than pouring from a bottle, so air-fried food turns golden and crisp without going soggy or greasy. Buyers say the sprayer is what finally made their air fryer results match the photos — a light coat on pakoras, kebabs and fries, instead of puddles of oil in the basket. For a small family watching oil use, buyers say that control is the whole point.

Can I use any oil in a glass oil sprayer, or only olive oil?

Buyers say most thin cooking oils work well — olive oil, avocado oil and vegetable oil are the usual choices — but very thick or cloudy oils can spray less evenly, so buyers suggest a quick, firm pump and a clean nozzle. Buyers also warn against aerosol cooking sprays in non-stick air fryer baskets, because many manufacturers advise that aerosol propellants can leave a sticky film on the coating. A refillable glass sprayer avoids that problem entirely, buyers say, because you fill it with your own plain oil.
