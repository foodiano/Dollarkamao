How I Use It in Foodiano World (The Desi Twist)

On my YouTube channel Foodiano World, the smoothest daal wins the table — and this is where the desi twist comes in. The twist I love: cook your daal until soft, then blend it right in the pot with the Ultra-Stick on a low speed until it turns silky, then finish with your tarka of cumin, garlic and dried red chilli. No jug to wash, no hot soup poured across the kitchen. The same stick makes mint-coriander chutney in a small jug, smooths tomato-onion gravy for curries, whips raita, and froths sweet lassi with the whisk attachment.

A tip from buyer reviews: tilt the head slightly and keep it moving instead of holding it flat on the bottom of the pot — that is how you avoid suction and get an even blend. And start on a low speed with hot liquids, buyers say, so nothing splashes. For a small family, that is the difference between a weeknight daal that tastes restaurant-smooth and one that stays stubbornly chunky.
