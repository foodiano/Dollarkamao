How to Choose

Hot-air is the pick for health and speed; stovetop only if flavour is everything and you do not mind washing up. Spray popped corn very lightly with oil or butter first — seasoning sticks to dry popcorn poorly. Store kernels airtight or they go stale and stop popping.

FAQ

Is hot-air popcorn actually healthy?

Yes — plain air-popped popcorn is a whole grain, high in fibre, with about 30 calories per cup. It only becomes junk food when drowned in butter and salt.

Why do so many kernels stay unpopped?

Stale kernels. Popcorn needs ~14% moisture inside to explode. Buy kernels in sealed bags, store airtight, and use within a year.

How do I make seasoning stick?

Mist the hot popcorn very lightly with oil spray or melted butter first, then toss with seasoning while it is still warm. Dry seasoning on dry popcorn just falls to the bottom.
