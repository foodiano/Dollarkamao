FAQ

Is a mandoline slicer safe for a small family kitchen?

Buyers say yes — as long as you always use the food holder that comes with it. The OXO handheld mandoline's food holder grips the vegetable and keeps your fingers away from the blade, and it doubles as a blade cover for storage, so the sharp edge is never exposed in a drawer. Buyers say a cut-resistant glove adds extra safety for beginners. Keep the slicer out of children's reach and always store it with the cover on.

What can I slice with a mandoline slicer?

Buyers say a mandoline handles onions, cucumbers, potatoes, carrots, zucchini, tomatoes and apples — anything firm that you want in thin, even slices. It is perfect for paper-thin onion rings for biryani, cucumber for raita, and potato slices for chips. Very soft foods like ripe tomatoes can tear at the thinnest setting, buyers say, so use the 2.5mm or 4mm setting for those and always finish with the food holder.

How do I clean a mandoline slicer?

Buyers say the OXO handheld mandoline is dishwasher safe, but hand-washing is simple: rinse it under running water right after use and brush the blade side carefully from the back, never toward the edge. Let it dry fully, then clip the food holder over the blade as a cover before putting it in a drawer. Buyers say never to wipe the blade with a bare cloth — the steel stays razor sharp even after years of use.
