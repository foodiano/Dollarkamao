Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Foodiano World, accessible at foodiano.vercel.app, your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this site and how it is used.

This site does not require you to create an account. If you contact us through the contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you provide, and we use it only to respond to you. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies

Like most websites, Foodiano World may use cookies to improve your experience — for example, to remember basic preferences or to understand which pages visitors read. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. The DART cookie enables Google and its partners to show you ads based on your browsing activity. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Links

Pages on this site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their policies.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach us at foodiano@gmail.com or through the contact page.
