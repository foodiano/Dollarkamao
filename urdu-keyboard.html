Use it in WhatsApp, Messages — any app

  1. Put it on your Home Screen like an app: tap the Share button in your browser, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen with its own icon, right beside your other apps.
  2. Speak here — tap the mic, say it in Urdu, and the Urdu script appears above. (If your phone's own dictation gives you Roman letters like “Assalamu Alaikum kya haal hai”, this page turns it into السلام علیکم کیا حال ہے.)
  3. Tap Copy (or Send to an App to pick WhatsApp, Messages, Mail… directly).
  4. Paste it in: open the other app, tap in its message box, and tap Paste. Done.

One honest note: only apps from the App Store can plug into the keyboard itself — a web page can't. This page is the closest thing: it lives on your Home Screen like an app, and copy / send works in every app on your phone.

How it works: voice runs through your browser's built-in speech recognition (set to Urdu). Anything it hears in Roman letters — or anything you type in the Type tab — is converted to Urdu script on this page. If the mic is blocked, allow microphone access for this page, or use the Type tab, which needs no mic at all.
